      Questions people ask before booking

      Can't see yours? Ask it on the call.

      Will AI replace my staff?

      No. I automate moving data, reminders, drafts and reports, and set up the operational processes behind them. People still make the decisions about money and staff, and your team gets time back for the work clients pay for. I don't take on projects aimed at cutting jobs.

      How much does it cost?

      The consultation is €45 for 30 minutes, and it comes off the implementation price. An audit and implementation depend on your processes and systems, so I quote them after the consultation. The price is fixed before any work starts.

      Is it worth it if I'm self-employed?

      Yes, if invoices, messages, client bookings and paperwork take up at least a few hours of your week. In the consultation I'll tell you what can be automated quickly and cheaply.

      Is our data safe?

      I work in line with the GDPR and use models that process data in the EU and don't train on it. n8n can run on your own server. You grant access, and you can revoke it at any time.

      What about AI in recruitment and the EU AI Act?

      I automate candidate emails, interview scheduling, paperwork and keeping systems in sync. If AI helps assess candidates, the recruiter always makes the call, and we check the legal requirements together before launch.

      What if something breaks or changes?

      Every automation is documented and belongs to you. With ongoing support I keep an eye on changes in your systems and update the integrations before anything breaks.

      Do we need our own developer?

      No. You get documentation and training, and your team can handle simple changes itself.

      How long does implementation take?

      It depends on the process and the systems. We set the timeline for each stage after the consultation.

      Which languages do you work in?

      English, Polish and Ukrainian. I work remotely with teams across Poland and the EU.

      Which tools do you work with?

      HubSpot, Google Workspace, Jira and Confluence, Power BI, n8n, Make, Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and anything with an API. I look at others during the consultation.
