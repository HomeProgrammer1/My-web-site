What data I receive

I keep correspondence and form data while we work together and afterwards for as long as possible claims require. Accounting records are kept for 5 years, as Polish law requires.

Only with providers the service cannot run without: email, the booking calendar, the payment system and hosting. Before launch this section will list the exact services. I do not sell your data or pass it on for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability, and you can object to processing. Write to kontakt@mlohaza.com and I will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland.
