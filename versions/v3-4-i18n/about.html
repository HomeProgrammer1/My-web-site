Three rules

  1. A Human Gate at every step.Agents move data, write drafts and send reminders. A person approves anything that touches money or people. I call this step the Human Gate.
  2. I listen to the team first.I automate what people themselves call routine. That's why the automations get used afterwards.
  3. I report in hours returned.After the pilot you see the numbers: how many hours came back and how many people actually use the automation.

What I don't do

  • I don't take projects whose goal is to cut staff.
  • I don't promise results without a pilot.
  • I don't build automations that nobody will maintain.
