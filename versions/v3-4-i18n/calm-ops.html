The Calm Ops method

My own approach to AI automation of operational processes: I listen to the team, map the processes, remove what's unnecessary, automate, train people and report in hours returned.

      What people ask before a consultation

      If your question isn't here, ask it during the consultation.

      Will AI replace my people?

      No. I automate routine work: moving data, reminders, drafts, reports. People make the decisions about money and people, and the team gets time for the work clients value. I don't take projects whose goal is to cut staff.

      How much does it cost?

      The consultation costs €45 for 30 minutes and counts toward implementation. The cost of an audit and implementation depends on your processes and systems, so I name it after the consultation, and it is fixed before work starts.

      Does it make sense if I'm self-employed?

      Yes, if routine takes at least a few hours a week: invoices, messages, client bookings, documents. In the consultation I'll tell you what can be automated quickly and cheaply.

      Is it safe to share our data?

      I work in line with the GDPR. I use models that process data in the EU and don't train on your data. n8n can run on your own server. You grant access and can revoke it at any time.

      AI in recruiting and the EU AI Act?

      I automate the operational part: emails to candidates, scheduling, documents, syncing systems. If AI helps assess candidates, the recruiter always makes the decision, and we check the legal requirements together before launch.

      What if something breaks or changes?

      The flows are documented and belong to you. On ongoing support I watch for changes in your systems and update integrations ahead of time.

      Do we need our own developer?

      No. You get documentation and training. The team can make simple changes on its own.

      How long does implementation take?

      It depends on the process and the systems. We fix the timeline for each stage after the consultation.

      Which languages do you work in?

      Polish, Ukrainian and English. I work remotely across Poland and the EU.

      Which systems do you work with?

      HubSpot, Google Workspace, Jira and Confluence, Power BI, n8n, Make, Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and any system with an API. I assess new ones during the consultation.
